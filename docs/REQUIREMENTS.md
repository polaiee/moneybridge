# Technica 2026 — Requirements and Relevant Challenges

(Excerpted from the Devpost page. Only the general rules and the challenges relevant to MoneyBridge are included.)

## General Requirements

- A project may be submitted to a maximum of 3 challenges, excluding a maximum of 3 sponsored challenges. A project may only win one challenge, excluding sponsored challenges.
- Virtual hackers must attend Expo through Gather and demo what they built to be eligible for prizes. Expo demos begin Sunday afternoon at 1:00 pm EST.
- Winners are announced at closing ceremony (4:00 pm EST on Sunday).
- Submissions are only valid if worked on within the 24-hour hacking period at Technica: noon on Saturday, October 17, 2026 until noon on Sunday, October 18, 2026.
- Cross-submissions from other hackathons are not accepted, and a Technica project may not be cross-submitted to other hackathons.

## Judging Criteria

1. **Adherence to Technica's Mission** — How does the project support an inclusive tech community? Does it consider accessibility, representation, user safety, empowerment, or other design choices that reflect Technica's mission? (Evaluates the project itself, not the identities of team members.)
2. **UI/UX Design** — How visually appealing and user-friendly is the interface/experience?
3. **Functionality & Execution** — Does the project work as intended?
4. **Originality & Creativity** — How creative is the idea or its execution?
5. **Adherence to Category** — How well does the project align with the category?

## Challenges MoneyBridge Targets

### [T. Rowe Price] NextGen Finance Innovation Challenge
Prizes: (4) Portable Monitors

The financial industry is being reshaped by exchange-traded funds (ETFs), artificial intelligence (AI), and digital assets and tokenization. This challenge asks teams to identify a real financial problem and show how one or more of these technologies could be used to solve it responsibly.

**Your Mission:** Create a prototype, tool, experience, or concept that helps users understand a financial problem and demonstrates a practical technology-driven solution. Teams may focus on one area or combine multiple areas below.

- **ETFs:** Fund comparison, active/passive strategies, fees, exposures, portfolio construction, and investor use cases.
- **Digital Assets and Tokenization:** Tokenized funds or assets, settlement, smart contracts, custody, and systems that support how digital financial products are issued, transferred, stored, or tracked.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Research, personalization, risk discovery, financial education, fraud awareness, and workflow efficiency.

**Project Ideas**
- AI-enabled ETF allocation simulator that helps users compare fund types, costs, exposures, risks, and portfolio trade-offs.
- Tokenization use-case simulator showing how a fund or asset could be issued, transferred, settled, or tracked digitally while highlighting regulation, custody, security, liquidity, and trust challenges.

**Key Questions**
- What user need or financial problem does the solution address?
- How is the technology applied in a realistic financial setting?
- What risks, limitations, or adoption challenges should users understand?
- How does the solution build trust through transparency, accessibility, and responsible design?

**Judging Criteria**
- **Impact and Application:** Addresses a meaningful financial use case and demonstrates practical value.
- **Innovation:** Presents a creative, forward-looking idea relevant to next-generation finance.
- **Clarity and Accuracy:** Explains financial and technical concepts clearly without overstating benefits or minimizing risks.
- **Accessibility:** Is simple, inclusive, intuitive, and understandable for users with different levels of financial knowledge.
- **Feasibility and Responsible Design:** Shows a realistic implementation path while addressing privacy, security, bias, regulatory constraints, transparency, and human oversight.

### [MLH] Best Use of Backboard
Prize: Tile Essentials Pack

Every AI model API is stateless by default. That means your app forgets everything the second a session ends. State management should be the first step of any AI build, regardless of what LLM you are using, and Backboard gives you that for free. But Backboard is not just state management. It is a single, unified API built on the world's #1 AI memory that gives you everything you need in one place: long-term memory, RAG, embeddings, tool calls, model routing across 17,000+ LLMs, and persistent context that stays alive across every page refresh, session, and user. No stitching together five different services. One API. One integration.

Example ideas: an AI travel guide that remembers allergies and preferences from past trips; a personalized fitness coach that adjusts workouts based on progress and injury history; a smart home controller that learns routines over time.

Use Backboard to build a seamless, persistent user experience without the usual infrastructure headache.
